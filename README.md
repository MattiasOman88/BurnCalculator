# Workout Log

Workout Log is a modern fitness tracking app designed to make training more motivating through gamification. Instead of only logging workouts, users earn XP, level up, unlock achievements, and track their long-term progress.

Whether you train at the gym, practice MMA, grappling, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, running, or other sports, Workout Log helps you stay consistent and motivated.

---

## Features

* 💪 Log workouts and exercises
* 📊 Detailed training statistics
* ⚖️ Weight tracking
* 🔥 XP and Level system
* 🏆 Achievement and badge system
* 🎯 Personal records
* 📅 Training streaks
* 📈 Progress tracking
* 💾 Backup and restore functionality
* 🌙 Modern and user-friendly interface

---

## Why Workout Log?

Many fitness apps focus only on recording workouts. Workout Log adds motivation through a game-inspired progression system.

Every workout contributes toward:

* Earning experience (XP)
* Reaching new levels
* Unlocking achievements
* Completing challenges
* Building long-term consistency

The goal is to make fitness enjoyable while helping users maintain healthy habits.

---

## Getting Started

### Requirements

* Android device (Android 8.0 or newer)
* Internet connection (only required for updates and optional cloud features)

### Installation

1. Download the app from Google Play (coming soon).
2. Create your profile.
3. Start logging workouts.
4. Earn XP and unlock achievements.
